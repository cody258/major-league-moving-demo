Questions customers ask before they book

What areas do you serve?

We're based in Abington and cover Greater Philadelphia, including Glenside, Jenkintown, Willow Grove, Blue Bell, Lansdale, Conshohocken and the city itself. We also handle moves from Pennsylvania into New Jersey.

How much does a move cost?

It depends on how much you're moving, how far, stairs and access, and whether you want us to pack. Tell us about your move and we'll give you a clear quote before you book, with no surprises on moving day.

Do you move pianos and other heavy items?

Yes. We've moved pianos out of basements and up flights of stairs, along with safes, gym equipment and other especially heavy pieces. Let us know what you have when you request a quote so we bring the right gear.

Can you pack everything for me?

Yes. We can pack your whole home or just the fragile items, and we bring wardrobe boxes for closets. You can also pack yourself and have us handle the heavy lifting.

Do you handle office and commercial moves?

Yes. We move offices and small businesses, including furniture disassembly and reassembly, and we'll work around your schedule to keep downtime short.

Can you haul away things I don't want to take?

Yes. We can remove furniture, junk and leftover items on the same trip, so you're not stuck dealing with them after the move.

How far ahead should I book?

The earlier the better, especially for weekends and the end of the month. If you're in a pinch, call anyway. We've squeezed in plenty of short-notice moves.
